Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us recognize you and improve your experience. We use both session-based and persistent cookies on our Service.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Service to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Service
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track and deliver targeted advertising

Other Tracking Technologies

We may also use:

  • Web beacons and pixels to track page visits and user interactions
  • Local storage and similar technologies
  • Analytics tools to monitor website performance

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, blocking cookies may affect the functionality of our Service. For more information about cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols
  • Secure socket layer (SSL) technology for all data transmissions
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy practices
  • Incident response procedures for potential data breaches

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and any transmission of data is at your own risk.
