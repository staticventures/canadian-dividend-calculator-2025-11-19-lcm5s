About DividendCalc

Founded in 2020, DividendCalc was created with a simple mission: to demystify Canadian dividend taxation and empower employers and investors with accurate financial planning tools. Our founding team recognized a critical gap in the market—there was no simple, reliable way for Canadians to calculate their net dividend income after accounting for complex provincial tax variations and dividend tax credits. We set out to solve this problem with cutting-edge technology and an unwavering commitment to accuracy.

Today, DividendCalc stands as Canada's most trusted dividend calculator, serving over 50,000 users including employers, financial advisors, accountants, and individual investors. Our core values center on transparency, accuracy, and accessibility. We believe that everyone deserves access to professional-grade financial planning tools without complex jargon or hidden fees. Our team continuously updates our calculations to reflect the latest Canadian tax laws and regulations, ensuring that every result you receive is current and reliable. We're committed to helping Canadians make smarter financial decisions and maximize their wealth through informed dividend planning.
